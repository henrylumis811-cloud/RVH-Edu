# RVH Edu V44 — Assessment policy review

- Added a dedicated, read-only Assessment Policy Review screen for the selected class, year, term, and section.
- The review identifies missing/legacy snapshots, invalid saved policies, and conflicting or mixed policy sets at learner level.
- Class results and analytics failures now route to the review screen rather than leaving the teacher with only a transient generic status message.
- Review is diagnostic only: it does not change marks, assessment states, or historical policy snapshots.
- Added a source regression check for screen wiring and read-only behavior.

Validation is source-level only unless a full Android Gradle build and device test are separately reported.
