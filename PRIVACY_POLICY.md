# RVH Edu Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026  
**Contact:** henrylumis811@gmail.com

RVH Edu is an offline-first school record and grading application. This policy describes the first-release design. The published policy and Play Console Data safety answers must be rechecked against the exact release build before publication.

## Information stored on your device

RVH Edu can store school details, learner details, enrolments, assessment marks, grading settings, academic history and other information you enter. These records are used to provide the app's school-management, grading, reporting and history features. They are stored locally on the device unless you choose to export, share or otherwise transmit them.

## No RVH-operated feedback or analytics server in this release

The first release does not have a configured RVH feedback server or audience-analytics endpoint. The app must not be described as sending learner records to an RVH server. Custom audience check-ins are disabled in the release workflow. Google Play may provide its own installation and app-performance statistics to the developer through Play Console.

## Email and WhatsApp feedback

If you choose an email or WhatsApp feedback action, RVH Edu prepares a message and opens another app or browser. You review and send it yourself. The selected provider then processes the message under its own terms and privacy practices. Do not include learner names, marks, contact details or confidential school records in feedback messages.

## Backups and exports

RVH Edu provides a user-initiated backup and restore workflow. You choose the destination for an exported backup. A backup may contain sensitive school and learner records; protect it, share it only with authorised people, and store it somewhere secure. The app's Android automatic backup rules exclude its private database, preferences and files so records are not silently transferred through Android backup. This does not replace the app's explicit backup feature.

## Network and updates

Core grading and recordkeeping are designed to work offline. The first release does not use a configured RVH-hosted update manifest or audience endpoint. When you choose to check for updates or open a feedback provider, the relevant external service or app may use network information such as an IP address under its own policies.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them, clear app data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove them. Exported backups are separate files and are not deleted by removing records from the app. Keep a verified backup before destructive actions. If you send feedback, retention of that message is governed by the email or messaging provider and your own account.

## Children's and learner information

Schools and teachers are responsible for entering information they are authorised to manage and for complying with applicable education and data-protection requirements. Use only the minimum information necessary and secure the device and any exported backups.

## Security

Use the device's screen lock, keep Android updated, restrict access to the app and protect exported backups. No local-storage or backup method should be assumed to be encrypted unless the device or storage mechanism explicitly provides that protection.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The current policy should be published at a stable public webpage and linked both from Google Play and inside RVH Edu. For privacy questions, contact **henrylumis811@gmail.com**.
